BELCH

Support for the BELCH app for Messages.

Open BELCH

  1. Open a conversation in Messages.
  2. Tap the + button, choose More, then select BELCH.
  3. Tap the giant green BELCH button and press Send.

Troubleshooting

If BELCH does not appear after installation, close and reopen Messages, then check the More list again. Make sure your device volume is turned up to hear the sound.
