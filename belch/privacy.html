BELCH Privacy Policy

Last updated July 22, 2026.

No data collection

BELCH does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. It does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

On-device operation

BELCH uses bundled audio and video files on your device. A small local preference may be stored to shuffle sounds and reduce immediate repeats. This information stays on your device.

Messages

When you choose to send a BELCH, Apple’s Messages app handles delivery to the recipients you select. BELCH does not operate a server that receives your conversations or recipient information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be posted on this page.
